About the Artist

Meet Kerri Guthrie

If you have ever looked at a painting and felt something settle deep in your chest, something warm and quiet and hard to name, then you already understand what Kerri Guthrie is trying to do with her art. Kerri is the artist, mother, and heart behind Angel's Artwork, and she has spent years creating pieces that reach people on a level that goes beyond decoration.

Based in South Jordan, Utah, Kerri works from a small studio where faith and creativity share the same space. She is not the kind of artist who paints for galleries or trends. She paints because she believes there is something real and true about the angels who walk beside us, and she wants to help people see that.

As a devoted mother, Kerri's life has always revolved around family. That shows up in her work constantly. You will find children surrounded by protective guardians, couples sheltered by heavenly figures, and families wrapped in light. Her art reflects the same love she pours into her own home, and that sincerity is what makes her paintings resonate with so many people.

The Ministry of Angels Series

Kerri's most recognized body of work is the Ministry of Angels series. These paintings depict everyday moments of life with a layer of divine presence woven in. A young girl reading her scriptures while an angel watches over her shoulder. A boy at prayer with a guardian standing close. A family holding hands beneath a sky filled with heavenly protectors.

What makes the Ministry of Angels series stand out is how natural it all feels. These are not dramatic, overwhelming scenes of the supernatural. They are quiet, tender moments. The kind of moments you might experience in your own living room or on a walk with your kids, except that Kerri pulls back the curtain and shows you what faith tells us is already there.

The series has grown over time and now includes pieces like Ministry of Angels, Guarded in Light, Reading Scriptures, and the beloved Robertson Family commission. Each piece carries the same gentle thread of protection and devotion.

The Spirit World Collection

Alongside the Ministry of Angels, Kerri has developed the Spirit World collection. These works explore the unseen realm more directly, painting scenes of what life beyond the veil might look like. Pieces like Through the Veil, Closer to Heaven, and Tree of Life invite viewers to sit with questions about eternity, reunion, and the connections that do not end when this life does.

The Spirit World collection tends to carry a different energy than the Ministry of Angels paintings. Where the angel works are grounded in daily life, these pieces open up something broader and more expansive. They are for the people who have lost someone they love, or for anyone who has ever wondered what waits on the other side. Kerri paints these with a deep tenderness, and it shows.

Bookmarks and Smaller Works

Not every piece Kerri creates is a large-format painting. She also designs a line of illustrated bookmarks that feature her angel imagery in a portable, everyday format. These make wonderful gifts and are a simple way to carry a reminder of faith and protection wherever you go.

Her Process and Medium

Kerri works primarily in digital painting, a medium that gives her the flexibility to build up layers of light and detail that would be difficult to achieve with traditional methods alone. Once a painting is complete, she prints each piece on premium archival paper. This is not your standard photo print. Archival paper is designed to hold color and detail for decades, so when you hang a Kerri Guthrie piece in your home, it is going to look just as vivid years from now as the day you first unwrapped it.

Her process usually starts with a feeling or a scene from scripture that stays with her. She sketches loosely, builds the composition digitally, and then refines the painting over many sessions. Kerri has said that some of her best work comes from the pieces she did not plan too carefully, the ones where she let the Spirit guide the brush more than her own intentions.

Commissions and Custom Work

One of the most meaningful parts of Kerri's work is the custom commissions she takes on. If you have ever wanted a painting of your own family surrounded by angels, or a personalized piece of religious art for someone you love, Kerri would be glad to talk with you about it. The Robertson Family piece is a beautiful example of what a custom commission looks like, a family portrait woven together with guardian angels and personal meaning.

To start a conversation about a commission, you can reach out through the contact form on the main site or email Kerri directly at kerrikreations@gmail.com.

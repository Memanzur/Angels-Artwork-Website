Ministry of Angels

Ministry of Angels - Boy

A companion piece to the original Ministry of Angels, this painting shows a young boy deep in his scripture study. Guardian angels surround him, their presence marked by a warm golden glow. The boy's concentration is complete, absorbed in words that carry weight beyond his understanding. The angels watch with expressions of care and purpose.

Artist's Inspiration

After completing the original girl version, Kerri received many requests from families wanting a version featuring a boy. She wanted to show that divine protection and spiritual guidance know no boundaries.

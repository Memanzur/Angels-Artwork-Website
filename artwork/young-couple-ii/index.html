Young Couple II

A second interpretation of the young couple theme, this variation offers a different composition while carrying the same message of faith-centered love under divine protection. The angels in this version are positioned differently, creating a more intimate atmosphere.

Artist's Inspiration

Due to the popularity of the original Young Couple piece, Kerri created this variation to offer couples a different perspective on the same beautiful theme of spiritually guarded love.

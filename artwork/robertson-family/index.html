Ministry of Angels

Robertson Family

A commissioned piece created for the Robertson family, this painting places their family portrait within the context of angelic guardianship. Each family member is shown with their own guardian angel, creating a deeply personal and meaningful work of art.

Artist's Inspiration

The Robertson family approached Kerri wanting a piece that reflected their family's faith and the spiritual protection they felt in their daily lives. This commissioned work became one of Kerri's most personal projects.

Ministry of Angels

Family Holding Hands

An entire family stands together, hands clasped, encircled by guardian angels. The composition emphasizes unity and shared faith. The angels form a protective ring, their wings creating a canopy of safety over the family below.

Artist's Inspiration

Family is central to everything Kerri creates. This piece was born from her belief that when families come together in faith, heaven draws especially close. She painted it as a reminder that spiritual bonds strengthen earthly ones.

Ministry of Angels

Angels with Shields & Swords

This powerful piece depicts a family in scripture study while armored angels stand guard with shields and swords. Unlike Kerri's softer works, this painting shows the warrior aspect of divine protection, with angels ready to defend against any threat to the family's spiritual well-being.

Artist's Inspiration

Kerri wanted to explore the idea of spiritual warfare and the active defense that guardian angels provide. She drew on scriptural descriptions of angels as warriors, not just messengers, to create this more dramatic interpretation.

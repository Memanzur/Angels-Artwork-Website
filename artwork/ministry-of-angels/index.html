Ministry of Angels

Ministry of Angels

This piece captures a young girl absorbed in reading her scriptures, completely unaware of the guardian angels standing beside her. Kerri Guthrie painted this work to remind viewers that we are never truly alone in our moments of devotion. The soft light pouring over the scene represents divine presence, while the angels stand with quiet strength, their wings partially unfurled in a protective embrace.

Artist's Inspiration

Kerri was inspired by watching her own children read their scriptures at home. She wanted to capture that tender moment when a child connects with something greater than themselves, and the unseen spiritual protection that surrounds those moments of faith.

Closer to Heaven

One of Kerri's most beloved works, Closer to Heaven depicts a joyous reunion in the next life. Figures embrace in a setting of pure light, the joy on their faces unmistakable. The painting captures the promise that those we love are not lost but waiting in a place of perfect happiness.

Artist's Inspiration

This piece came from Kerri's deep conviction that families are eternal. She painted it as a gift of comfort for anyone who has lost someone dear, showing the reunion that awaits on the other side.

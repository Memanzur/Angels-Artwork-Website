Ministry of Angels

Young Couple

Two young people sit side by side, reading together, while angels keep watch over their shared devotion. The painting captures the early days of a relationship built on faith, with heaven approving and protecting their union.

Artist's Inspiration

Kerri created this for young couples starting their journey together. She wanted to show that when two people build their relationship on a foundation of faith, they invite divine companionship into their partnership.

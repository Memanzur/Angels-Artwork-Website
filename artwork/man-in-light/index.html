Man in Light

A solitary figure stands engulfed in divine radiance. The light is not external but seems to emanate from within and around simultaneously. This piece explores the transformative power of divine presence, showing a person in the moment of spiritual awakening.

Artist's Inspiration

Kerri painted this piece to represent the moments in life when the divine presence becomes overwhelmingly real. She wanted to show that divine encounters are not distant or abstract but intensely personal and transformative.

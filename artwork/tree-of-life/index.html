Tree of Life

The Tree of Life rises from divine hands, its branches reaching toward an endless sky. This piece draws on deep scriptural symbolism, depicting the tree as a source of eternal nourishment and truth. Light emanates from the fruit, casting a warm glow across the entire composition.

Artist's Inspiration

Kerri drew on the vision of the Tree of Life described in scripture, wanting to create a visual representation that would make the abstract feel tangible. She spent weeks refining the balance of light and form.

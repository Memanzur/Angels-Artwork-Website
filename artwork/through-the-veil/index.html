Spirit World

Through the Veil

One of Kerri's most profound works, Through the Veil depicts the sacred moment when the boundary between this world and the next becomes thin. Figures reach across the divide, suggesting reunion, hope, and the continuity of love beyond mortality.

Artist's Inspiration

Kerri painted this after the passing of a close family member. The piece became a way to process grief while affirming her belief that death is not an ending but a passage. It has since become one of her most requested works by families experiencing loss.

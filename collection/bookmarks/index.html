Angel Bookmarks Collection

Illustrated angel bookmarks for scripture study, gifts, and sharing. Sold in packs of ten.

Not every piece of art needs a frame and a wall. Sometimes the best place for a beautiful image is right there between the pages of the book you are reading. Kerri Guthrie's Angel Bookmarks collection takes the same guardian angel imagery from her full-size paintings and distills it into a format that you can hold in your hand, tuck into a scripture, or slip into a greeting card for someone who needs a little encouragement. These bookmarks are printed on sturdy cardstock with rich, vibrant color, and they are sold in packets of ten so you always have extras to share.

The idea for the bookmarks came from Kerri's experience at art shows and church events. People loved her Ministry of Angels prints but did not always have the wall space or budget for a framed piece. They kept asking for something smaller, something they could give to a Sunday School class, hand out at a baptism, or include in a care package. The bookmarks were the answer. They carry the same emotional weight and spiritual meaning as the larger paintings, just in a pocket-sized format that fits naturally into the daily rhythm of reading and study.

Four Editions, One Message

The collection currently includes four distinct editions. The Blonde, Brunette, and Latino editions each feature a young person reading their scriptures, watched over by guardian angels, with the figure's appearance reflecting different backgrounds and hair colors. Kerri designed it this way on purpose. She wants every child, teenager, and young adult to be able to see themselves in the artwork. The Classic edition, titled "Minister of Angels Bookmark," uses a more universal composition that works for any audience.

Each edition is sold in packets of ten identical bookmarks. That pack size makes them ideal for group settings. Teachers use them as class gifts. Parents hand them out at birthday parties. Youth leaders include them in activity kits. They also work well as stocking stuffers, Easter basket additions, or simple "thinking of you" gifts that carry more meaning than a generic card from the store.

Practical Details

The bookmarks are printed on heavyweight cardstock with a smooth, semi-gloss finish that resists bending and wear. They are standard bookmark size, designed to fit comfortably in any book without sticking out too far. The artwork is printed on one side with a clean, simple back. All four editions are available through the Square online shop, where you can order as many packets as you need. Shipping is handled directly through Square, and bulk orders for events or organizations can be arranged by contacting Kerri directly.

If you love the bookmark designs, you will want to see the full-size paintings they are drawn from. The Ministry of Angels collection includes all of the original works that inspired these bookmarks, available as prints in sizes ranging from 8x10 to 24x36.

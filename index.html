Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the artist behind Angel's Artwork?

Kerri Guthrie is an inspired artist based in South Jordan, Utah. She creates original angel-themed artwork including prints, bookmarks, and commissioned pieces that bring comfort and a touch of the divine into everyday spaces.

What type of artwork does Angel's Artwork offer?

Angel's Artwork specializes in original angel-themed paintings and prints, including the Ministry of Angels series, Spirit World collection, and illustrated bookmarks. Each piece captures moments of heavenly grace and divine protection.

How can I purchase artwork?

You can purchase prints, bookmarks, and other artwork through our Square online shop. Custom commissions are also available by contacting us directly through the contact form.

Where is Angel's Artwork located?

Angel's Artwork is located at 8822 South Redwood Road, South Jordan, UT 84088. We are open Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Does Angel's Artwork offer custom commissions?

Yes! Kerri Guthrie accepts custom commission requests for personalized angel-themed artwork. Contact us through the website form or email kerrikreations@gmail.com to discuss your vision.

the artist

Kerri Guthrie

Kerri Guthrie is an inspired artist and devoted mother. Her passion and vision is evident in her work. Each piece carries a sense of grace, capturing moments that feel both heavenly and deeply personal.

Based in South Jordan, Utah, Kerri creates original angel-themed artwork that brings comfort, beauty, and a touch of the divine into everyday spaces.
